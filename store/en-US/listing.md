# en-US

## Title (30)
Bingo: party with friends

## Short description (80)
Your phone becomes the bingo cage and guests play on their phones. No internet.

## Full description (4000)
Turn any phone into a bingo cage and bring family, friends or coworkers together in seconds. Bingo runs on the local Wi-Fi network, with no internet and no sign-up: the host creates a room, guests scan the QR code, and everyone gets a card on their own phone.

HOW IT WORKS
• The host taps "Create room" and names the game.
• Guests join by scanning the QR code or opening the link shared on WhatsApp.
• The host draws balls, shown with big letters and numbers, and everyone sees the last ball called.
• Whoever completes a card shouts BINGO in the app and the host checks it instantly.

MADE FOR PARTIES
• Works with local Wi-Fi only, even on a router without internet or a phone hotspot.
• Cards on every guest's phone: no paper, no pens, no lost cards.
• Have a physical cage? Use manual mode and type the ball that came out.
• History of drawn balls, undo the last draw and start a new round with one tap.
• Solo mode to practice.

PREMIUM, WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Rooms with unlimited players.
• Auto cage: the app draws by itself at the pace you choose.
• No ads.
Free for rooms up to 10 players. Weekly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Cancel anytime from your Google Play account.

PRIVACY
No account, no sign-up, no location data. Games happen only between phones on the same network.

Languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian and Japanese.
